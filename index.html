          About Nagpur Happenings

          Nagpur Happenings is an independent calendar of offline things to do in Nagpur: book clubs, board-game and chess meetups, open mics, theatre, music, workshops, talks, walks and community gatherings.

          Organisers submit their events, and each one is reviewed before it is listed. Details can change, so check with the organiser before you head out.
