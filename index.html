          About Nagpur Happenings

          Nagpur Happenings is an independent calendar of offline things to do in Nagpur: book clubs, board-game and chess meetups, open mics, theatre, music, workshops, talks, walks and community gatherings.

          Organisers submit their events, and each one is reviewed before it is listed. Details can change, so check with the organiser before you head out.

          It starts at Zero Mile, in the Orange City and Tiger Capital of India. Nagpur first, more cities soon.

          What gets listed

          • Events in Nagpur that anyone can turn up to, with a date, a place (or an online link) and a way to join.
          • Book clubs, quizzes, games, workshops, walks, talks, performances, markets and community meetups. Free and paid.
          • Not listed: courses and admissions, product sales and offers, or anything without a date.
          • Every submission is checked before it goes live. We may shorten or correct details, or decline a listing.
          • Organisers are responsible for their events. Please confirm details with them before you go or pay.

          Privacy

          • You don't need an account or a login to browse.
          • Visits and clicks are counted with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or follow you across other websites.
          • When you submit an event, your private email or phone is used only to reach you about it. It is never shown on the site, shared or sold. The public contact you choose is shown with your event.
          • Poster uploads go through Google Forms, which asks you to sign in with Google.
          • To change or remove anything you've sent, email community@arinjay.email.
