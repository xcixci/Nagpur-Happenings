Nagpur Happenings category icons
================================

svg-website/   Uses currentColor, so the website can colour them with CSS.
svg-green/     Brand green #0E451C, ready for Canva, posters and reels.
png-green-512/ 512 x 512 px PNGs on a transparent background.

cat-*  = the 8 categories on the website.
sub-*  = draft subcategories, for reels, posters and future filters.

Style: Tabler outline icons, 24 x 24 grid, 1.75 stroke, rounded ends.
